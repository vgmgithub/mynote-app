Each card is one company: how many people follow it this week, what the archive holds, and the articles themselves. Quiet is the list worth watching - companies people follow that came back with nothing today. Requests today on the tiles above counts every call to the news provider, including refused ones, because that is what the provider counts against its daily allowance. Follower counts are people, not requests, and never say who follows what.

These cards read the archive the way a phone's Feed reads it, with one difference worth knowing: the app also filters each article against the user's own typed holding name before scoring it, and nothing here can do that. So this answers "what did we collect for this company", not "what one particular person sees".
